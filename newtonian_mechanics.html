1.1

Sistema de referenciaReference frame

Sea $A$ un espacio afín con espacio director $E$, y sea $B$ una base de $E$. Un sistema de referencia es un par $(O,B)$ con $O\in A$; el punto $O$ se llama origen.

Let $A$ be an affine space with direction space $E$, and let $B$ be a basis of $E$. A reference frame is a pair $(O,B)$ with $O\in A$; the point $O$ is called the origin.

1.2

Coordenadas afinesAffine coordinates

Dado un sistema de referencia $(O,B)$ con $B=\{\e{1},\dots,\e{n}\}$ y un punto $P\in A$, existen únicos escalares $\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_n$ tales que

Given a reference frame $(O,B)$ with $B=\{\e{1},\dots,\e{n}\}$ and a point $P\in A$, there exist unique scalars $\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_n$ such that

\overrightarrow{OP}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_i\,\e{i}

Los escalares $\lambda_i$ se llaman las coordenadas de $P$.

The scalars $\lambda_i$ are called the coordinates of $P$.

2.1

Movimiento y posiciónMotion and position

\begin{array}{rccl}\v{r}\colon & \RR & \to & \RR^{3}\\ & t & \mapsto & \v{r}(t)\end{array}
2.2

Velocidad y aceleración mediasAverage velocity and acceleration

\v{v}_{\mathrm{m}}(t)=\frac{\Delta\v{r}}{\Delta t}
\v{a}_{\mathrm{m}}(t)=\frac{\Delta\v{v}}{\Delta t}
2.3

Velocidad instantáneaInstantaneous velocity

\v{v}(t)=\dv{\v{r}}{t}=\dot{\v{r}}(t)
\dot{\v{r}}=v\,\e{t}=\dv{s}{t}\,\e{t}
2.4

Aceleración instantáneaInstantaneous acceleration

\v{a}(t)=\dvv{\v{r}}{t}=\ddot{\v{r}}(t)
\ddot{\v{r}}=\dv{v}{t}\,\e{t}+\frac{v^{2}}{\rho}\,\e{n}
2.5

Curvatura y radio de curvaturaCurvature and radius of curvature

\kappa=\frac{1}{\rho}=\abs{\dv{\e{t}}{s}}
\rho=\frac{\abs{\dot{\v{r}}}^{3}}{\abs{\dot{\v{r}}\times\ddot{\v{r}}}}=\frac{v^{3}}{\abs{\v{v}\times\v{a}}}
\rho(x)=\frac{\bigl[1+y'(x)^{2}\bigr]^{3/2}}{\abs{y''(x)}}

La última expresión vale para una curva dada como $y=y(x)$.

The last expression holds for a curve given as $y=y(x)$.

3Coordenadas cilíndricasCylindrical coordinates

3.1

Cambio de coordenadasChange of coordinates

\begin{aligned}x&=R\cos\phi\\ y&=R\sin\phi\\ z&=z\end{aligned}
3.2

Matriz de cambio de baseChange-of-basis matrix

\begin{pmatrix}\e{R}\\ \e{\phi}\\ \e{z}\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\phi&\sin\phi&0\\ -\sin\phi&\cos\phi&0\\ 0&0&1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\e{x}\\ \e{y}\\ \e{z}\end{pmatrix}
3.3

Derivadas temporalesTime derivatives

\edot{R}=\dot{\phi}\,\e{\phi}
\edot{\phi}=-\dot{\phi}\,\e{R}
3.4

Posición, velocidad y aceleraciónPosition, velocity and acceleration

\begin{aligned} \v{r}&=R\,\e{R}+z\,\e{z}\\[4pt] \dot{\v{r}}&=\dot{R}\,\e{R}+R\dot{\phi}\,\e{\phi}+\dot{z}\,\e{z}\\[4pt] \ddot{\v{r}}&=\bigl(\ddot{R}-R\dot{\phi}^{2}\bigr)\e{R}+\bigl(R\ddot{\phi}+2\dot{R}\dot{\phi}\bigr)\e{\phi}+\ddot{z}\,\e{z} \end{aligned}
